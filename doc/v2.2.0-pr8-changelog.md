# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 8

## The House Always Wins

PR8 is a focused hardware-test follow-up to PR7.

### IL fixes

- Fixed Full Reds entering the normal secret instead of replay-secret mode.
  The temporary completed-main-Shine state now stays active until the secret
  has actually loaded, so FLUDD remains available and the separate Full Reds
  PB can be awarded.
- Kept ordinary Secret and Reds routes on their existing behaviour.
- Fixed Bianco 2's long-broken FMV split at the game's real handoff into the
  Windmill/Petey movie. The active route now survives that handoff, the FMV
  advances exactly once, and all three Petey hits continue to advance exactly
  once.
- Existing Bianco 2 attempts, finishes, overall PB, and rollout split are kept.
  Only the newly separated timing around the FMV starts fresh; every later IL
  record keeps its existing slot.

### Display fixes

- Separated Pinna 8's timer and balloon counter without changing the timer's
  timing or calculation code.
- Replaced Ricco 2's checkpoint wireframe cages with calmer transparent
  checkpoint volumes and retained the small checkpoint labels.
- Kept the near-camera safety checks that prevent practice visuals from
  stretching across or corrupting the screen.

### Testing scope

- The PR7 input, Dust, hurtbox, and menu-description changes are unchanged.
- Ghost race code is unchanged in PR8; its skipped Noki 6 Secret check is
  carried into the PR8 tester log.

The QFT timer implementation was not changed.
