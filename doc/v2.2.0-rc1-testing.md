This is a release check, not a clean-install test. Do not delete settings,
records, ghosts, or other SD data. The checklist is meant to be shared across
the tester group: nobody needs to repeat all 18 checks, and each tester only
needs their own game version. Please cover JP, US, and PAL at least once across
the whole group.

check number, game version, and one short sentence. A photo or short video is

## Basic release check

- [ ] **1. Boot and version:** Reach gameplay. The launcher says
  `V2.2.0 Release Candidate 1 "The House Always Wins"`, and Records says
  `Moonshine V2.2.0 RC1`.

- [ ] **2. Normal IL:** With assists off, finish one familiar short IL. The
  timer, splits, PB, Records result, and result message behave normally, with
  no red HUD or `INVALID IL` warning.

- [ ] **3. Menus:** Browse the main Settings pages. Descriptions remain
  readable, long pages scroll correctly, and C-stick section jumping works.
  Open Moonshine while a pause menu or save box is visible; only Moonshine
  should react until its menu is closed.

- [ ] **4. Quick savestate check:** Save and load once in an ordinary stage.
  Mario, nearby objects, coins, enemies, and controller state return correctly.

## ILs, Streaking, and ghosts

- [ ] **5. Full Reds:** Complete one Full Reds route. FLUDD remains available
  in the secret, the replay red-coin Shine appears, and the result is saved as
  Full Reds. Across the tester group, use the IL list and Stage Loader at least
  once each; one person does not need to do both.

- [ ] **6. Bianco 2:** Complete the Bianco 2 IL. Splits advance once each for
  the rollout, Windmill/Petey movie handoff, Petey hits 1, 2, and 3, followed
  by the final Shine. Failed stomps and ordinary movement must not add splits.

- [ ] **7. Streaking:** Hidden and 100-coin Shines appear in the Streaking
  list. A normal episode streak accepts another Shine collected in that same
  episode, while a Full Reds streak still requires the Full Reds finish.

- [ ] **8. Ghost race saving — one tester:** In Noki 6 Secret, race a saved
  ghost, finish a visibly different run, and save it. Reloading the challenger
  shows the complete new run, while the chosen opponent remains available to
  race again.

## RNG controls

- [ ] **9. King Boo:** Complete five roulette results with the control enabled:
  fruit, no fruit, fruit, no fruit, fruit. Aborting a no-fruit cycle must not
  grant the next fruit early.

- [ ] **10. Petey:** Enable both Petey controls in Bianco 5. Petey never uses
  the tornado and follows N1, S1, S2, S3. King Boo or Petey assistance turns
  the HUD red, keeps `INVALID IL` visible, and disables Layout Editor. Turning
  every boss control off restores the normal HUD immediately.

- [ ] **11. Ricco machinery:** Try all five crane speed choices and confirm
  each crane keeps its selected speed until restart. With `Durians only`, every
  fruit successfully launched by the fruit machine is a durian. `Retail`
  restores normal behaviour.

- [ ] **12. Eggs, fish, and birds — specialist:** Any fruit can open a Yoshi
  egg when enabled. A tester familiar with Gelato 6 fish and blue-coin birds
  should use `PATTERN_TESTING.md` for the forced patterns. These controls must
  not turn the HUD red.

## Displays and practice tools

- [ ] **13. Movement displays:** Rollout reports `1f` through `5f` and caps
  longer holds at `5f`. Dust reports normal dry-ground timings but nothing
  while Mario is water-sliding. Their Layout Editor pages still work.

- [ ] **14. Airgrab and input display:** Airgrab QFT freeze works only while
  stationary, empty-handed Mario holds B. The classic input display responds
  correctly to both sticks, buttons, and Start, including at its smallest size.

- [ ] **15. Assisted actions and position:** Position save/load restores Mario
  and the camera. Spawn Yoshi, Fruit Regrab, and a successful position load
  prevent that attempt from earning a PB but do not turn the HUD red. Merely
  saving a position does not invalidate the attempt.

- [ ] **16. Hidden items and hurtboxes:** Check hidden fruit/coin filters and
  labels in one stage outside Pinna; revealed items lose their markers. Check
  at least two hurtbox styles and turn them off again. A tester familiar with
  Eely-Mouth should check that live teeth appear without the eel's body covering
  the screen. Both displays prevent assisted PBs while enabled.

- [ ] **17. Ricco 2 checkpoints:** Leave checkpoints enabled for the whole
  race. Every visible checkpoint remains present continuously instead of
  appearing briefly and vanishing. They stay transparent and calm from near
  and far away, never stretch across the screen, and disappear when disabled.
  An attempt made with them enabled cannot earn a PB.

- [ ] **18. Pinna 8:** Watch the roller-coaster HUD through a full ride. The
  Moonshine timer stays above the balloon counter and both remain fully visible.
