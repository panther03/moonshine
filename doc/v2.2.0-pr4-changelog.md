# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 4

## The House Always Wins

- Bianco 2 now splits on the high rollout and Petey hits 1, 2, and 3. The unreliable FMV split and its hook are gone.
- Removed the unfinished Bianco Skeeter control. Its old saved-settings slot remains reserved, so existing INI files stay compatible.
- Rebuilt the Settings menu with clearer category names and nested pages for Gameplay, RNG, Timer, HUD, and Appearance.
- Renamed RNG rows so the result is obvious. Ricco now shows `Crane speed` and `Fruit machine`, and forced choices use `Retail` for normal behaviour.
- Blue-coin bird patterns now target blue-coin birds across courses instead of being restricted to Gelato 6. The Gelato 6 red-coin fish control remains separate.
- Kept the red HUD and `INVALID IL` warning limited to the Petey and King Boo assists. Spawn Yoshi and Fruit Regrab still invalidate assisted IL credit without changing the HUD.
- Preserved all later split-stat slots while retiring the Bianco 2 FMV segment, and added safe migration for both earlier V2.2.0 schemas.

Gameplay timer behaviour was not changed.
