# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 6 — tester log

## The House Always Wins

This is a focused check of the things changed after PR5. Mark each item **PASS**,
**FAIL**, or **NOT TESTED**. A short note, photo, or video is enough for a
failure. Skip anything you do not know how to test.

- [ ] **1. Boot and version:** Reach gameplay. The launcher says
  `V2.2.0 Pre-Release 6 "The House Always Wins"`, and Records says
  `Moonshine V2.2.0 PR6`.

- [ ] **2. IL list order:** `Gelato GBS` appears with Gelato in ILs, normal
  Stage Loader, and Streaking. Each course's new `Full Reds` route also appears
  with that course rather than at the bottom.

- [ ] **3. Streaking choices:** Hidden Shines and 100-coin Shines are available
  in the Streaking list again.

- [ ] **4. Full Reds:** Start one `Full Reds` route. It begins at the start of
  the normal episode, continues into its secret, makes the replay red-coin
  Shine available, and records as `Full Reds` when completed.

- [ ] **5. Full Reds streak rules:** A normal episode streak counts the full
  secret-red Shine from that episode. A streak explicitly set to `Full Reds`
  only counts the full-red route; grabbing the normal episode Shine must not
  shortcut it.

- [ ] **6. Bianco 2 splits:** Complete the Bianco 2 IL. Its five splits are, in
  order: high rollout, Petey hit 1, Petey hit 2, Petey hit 3, final Shine. Each
  Petey hit advances exactly once.

- [ ] **7. Dust near water:** Dust still reports dry-ground landings. It reports
  nothing after landing on wet ground, a normal waterslide, or FLUDD-created
  sliding water.

- [ ] **8. Tiny Start button:** Shrink the classic input display to minimum
  size. Start has a visible hollow centre at rest and becomes a clear solid
  3-by-3 mark while pressed.

- [ ] **9. Ghost race saving:** Choose a library ghost, select `RACE`, wait for
  `Ghost ready - restart to race`, then restart once. Finish a visibly different
  run and save it. Reloading that challenger should show its complete route and
  time, while the chosen opponent remains selected.

- [ ] **10. Hidden items:** Under HUD and Displays, try hidden-item choices
  `Both`, `Fruit`, and `Coins` in Pinna and at least one other stage. Only the
  chosen types appear. Turning labels off leaves the spot marker without the
  item name. Revealed items disappear individually.

- [ ] **11. Hidden-item IL warning:** Start an IL with hidden-item markers on.
  It is clearly treated as assisted and cannot earn a PB, Records result,
  split result, streak result, or playlist result.

- [ ] **12. Enemy hurtboxes:** Try `Wireframe`, `Transparent`, and `Solid`, then
  `Off`. Try both `All enemies` and `Eely teeth only`. In Eely-Mouth, live teeth
  remain visible but Eely's large body does not cover the screen; destroyed
  teeth disappear. Any enabled hurtbox display makes the attempt assisted.

- [ ] **13. Ricco 2 checkpoints:** Enable the Ricco race-checkpoint display and
  enter Ricco 2. The visible checkpoint volumes match the race gates and vanish
  immediately when disabled. Using them makes the attempt assisted.

- [ ] **14. Pinna 8 HUD:** In Pinna 8, the balloon count is readable and no
  longer sits under the Moonshine timer. The timer itself remains in its normal
  position and behaves normally.
