## Checks

- [ ] **1. Boot and version:** Reach gameplay. The launcher says
  `V2.2.0 Release Candidate 2 "The House Always Wins"`, and Records says
  `Moonshine V2.2.0 RC2`.

- [ ] **2. Noki ghost stress check:** Select a ghost in Noki 1, enter the
  stage, play for at least 20 seconds, then restart or reload the stage. Repeat
  this five times. The game must not crash, and the ghost should remain visible
  as either its 3D character or the simple ghost marker.

- [ ] **3. Other ghost check:** Use one familiar ghost race outside Noki. Race
  or watch it through one complete attempt. The ghost should move and draw
  normally, with no crash during the run or when leaving the stage.

- [ ] **4. Start symbol:** Turn on the classic input display and press Start.
  At both your normal display size and the smallest size, Start remains clear
  but looks smaller than the main face buttons.

- [ ] **5. Ordinary IL smoke test:** With practice assists off, finish one
  familiar short IL. Timing, splits, the result screen, and PB handling should
  behave normally.

- [ ] **6. General glance:** Open Settings and the Layout Editor once, then
  return to gameplay. Existing pages, descriptions, HUD elements, and controls
  should look and behave as they did in RC1.
