# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 2 "The House Always Wins"

Pre-Release 2 builds on the working Pre-Release 1 core. The launcher, existing
RNG controls, IL invalidation, and the other PR1 changes remain in place.

## Practice feedback and timing

- The `INVALID IL` warning is made clearly visible whenever the persistent
  Petey or King Boo controls are active.
- Rollout feedback shows how many effective frames A was held, from `1f` to
  `5f`. Longer holds remain capped at `5f`.
- Dust feedback shows the delay between landing and rolling out as `1st`
  through `6th`, then `Late`, using the existing wallkick-display presentation.
- QFT can freeze during Mario's stationary, empty-handed airgrab animation.

## Bianco controls and splits

- RNG Control gains a Bianco Skeeter choice with `Off`, `Left`, `Middle`, and
  `Right` presets for the Skeeter left of the windmill in Bianco 1 and 2. This
  control does not invalidate the IL, turn the HUD red, or lock Creation.
- The Bianco 2 IL cutscene trigger now produces its intended split once.

## Build identity

- Homebrew Channel and the launcher identify this build as **Moonshine Launcher
  ZETA** and **Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 2 "The House Always Wins"**.
- The in-game Records page identifies it as **Moonshine V2.2.0 PR2**.
- Durable storage and internal ABI names remain unchanged: keep `susamune.ini`
  and existing `susamune_*` files in place.
