# Moonshine V2.2.0 "The House Always Wins"

V2.2.0 is Moonshine's largest practice and achievement expansion yet. It adds
new RNG controls, Full Reds support, clearer practice feedback, safer PB
handling, improved ghost racing, and **59 new achievements**. The active
catalog now contains exactly **210 achievements**.

## Achievements

- Added 20 Times achievements: **Squid Pro Quo**, **Dock Knock**,
  **Uncorked**, **Chain Reaction**, **Goopy Business**, **Bombastic Balloons**,
  **Leaf Me Here**, **Praise the Sun**, **Hillside Heist**, **Triple Terror**,
  **Skipped Sumi**, **Stop, Thief!**, **I Have the High Ground!**,
  **Stop Right There, Criminal Scum**, **Security!!**,
  **By the Order of the Elder!**, **The House Always Wins**, **Mantastic**,
  **Perfect Peter**, and **The Culmination**.
- Added 15 Streaks achievements: **Rooted**, **Surf's Up**,
  **Hotel Loyalty**, **Brush Your Teeth**, **No Refunds**, **Clean Sweep**,
  **Thanks for My Gun**, **Round and Round**, **Scooby Dooby Doo**,
  **Robbing the Village**, **Baywatch**, **Pieces rouges de la grotte**,
  **A Certain Someone**, **He Loves Squids**, and **Motion Sickness**.
- Added six Challenges achievements: **Ruins Rampage**, **No Safety Net**,
  **A New Leaf**, **Orange's Quest**, **Lost and FLUDD**, and **Reef Runner**.
- Added five Course Mastery achievements: **Shadow Slayer**,
  **Plaza Beginnings**, **Plaza Specialist**, **Plaza Graduate**, and
  **Plaza Master**.
- Added 13 Special achievements: **By a Nose**, **Mirror Match**,
  **Stranger Danger**, **Dead Heat**, **New Kid on the Block**, **Upstart**,
  **Serious Business**, **Magnate**, **Industrialist**, **Robber Baron**,
  **Mogul**, **Tycoon**, and **Emperor**.
- Achievement popups now show the achievement's category as well as its tier.
- **The House Always Wins** permits only the matching King Boo control, while
  **Perfect Peter** permits only the matching Petey controls. Other assists
  still disqualify the attempt.
- Removed the duplicate achievements **Chuckster Change**, **No Kidding**, and
  **Tower Titan**. Existing unlocks migrate to their surviving equivalents.

## PB Safety

- Added **Settings > PB Safety**, a single place to see every saved setting
  that currently prevents IL PBs.
- The page shows how many blockers are active and provides **Fix** and
  **Fix all** shortcuts.
- PB-affecting choices are also marked on their normal Settings pages. An
  unsafe value is shown in red as **PB OFF**.
- Boss assistance remains deliberately unmistakable: the HUD turns red,
  Creation is locked, and **INVALID IL** stays visible while a Petey or King
  Boo control is active.
- Successful one-shot assists such as Spawn Yoshi, Fruit Regrab, and position
  loading invalidate competitive credit without forcing the red HUD.
- Hidden-item markers, enemy hurtboxes, and Ricco 2 checkpoint displays also
  prevent assisted PB and Records credit while enabled.

## RNG practice

- King Boo can use a repeatable **fruit, no fruit, fruit, no fruit** roulette
  cycle. A no-fruit round must be completed before the next fruit round is
  available.
- Bianco 5 Petey can be prevented from using tornadoes and sent through the
  authored **N1 -> S1 -> S2 -> S3** route.
- Ricco's five cranes can each receive a stable random speed from one of five
  bands: **Slowest**, **Slow**, **Medium**, **Fast**, or **Fastest**.
- Ricco's fruit machine can be set to launch **Durians only**.
- Added repeatable patterns for the Gelato 6 red-coin fish and blue-coin birds
  across the courses in which they appear.
- RNG Control is organised into smaller pages alongside Pattern Selector and
  Any Fruit Opens Yoshi Eggs.

## ILs, Streaking, and ghosts

- Added separate **Full Reds** ILs for all ten full-level secret-red routes:
  Bianco 3 and 6, Ricco 4, Gelato 1, Pinna 2 and 6, Sirena 2 and 4, Noki 6,
  and Pianta 5.
- Full Reds routes are available from ILs, Stage Loader, and Streaking. They
  keep FLUDD through the secret and record separately from ordinary Secret
  Reds.
- Bianco 2 now records the high rollout, the Windmill/Petey movie handoff,
  Petey's three hits, and the final Shine in order.
- Streaking can credit any Shine collected in the selected episode while
  keeping Full Reds routes exact. Hidden and 100-coin choices remain
  available.
- Fixed recording and saving a new challenger while racing a selected ghost,
  including child-stage routes such as Noki 6 Secret.
- Ghost achievements lock in the selected opponent, source, route, and
  starting PB when the race begins, preventing selection changes from
  awarding the wrong result.
- Added the classic position save and load actions to Button Binds.

## Practice displays and controls

- Added Rollout feedback for effective 1-5 frame A presses and Dust feedback
  for the delay between landing and rolling out. Both can be edited in the
  Layout Editor.
- Added a QFT-freeze option for Mario's stationary, empty-handed airgrab.
- Restored the classic input display, including a clearer Start indicator and
  its full Creation editor.
- Added global markers for spray-hidden fruit and coins, with Fruit, Coins,
  or Both filters and optional labels.
- Added enemy hurtboxes with Wireframe, Transparent, Solid, and Off styles,
  plus an Eely-teeth-only option.
- Added transparent race-checkpoint volumes for Ricco 2.
- Kept Pinna 8's balloon count clear of the Moonshine timer.

## Menus and presentation

- Reorganised Settings into smaller Gameplay, RNG, Timer, HUD, Appearance,
  and PB Safety pages with clearer names, separators, and descriptions.
- Added faster C-stick navigation to long menu pages. In Achievements,
  C-stick Left and Right jump directly between tiers.
- Moonshine now takes control of the pad while its menu is open, preventing
  inputs from reaching the pause menu, save box, or gameplay binds beneath it.

## Reliability and compatibility

- Fixed a crash that could occur while drawing a 3D ghost after repeated stage
  loads; an incomplete model now falls back to the simple ghost marker.
- Hardened savestate loading against damaged or stale snapshots, active
  memory-card work, and settings being silently rewound by an older state.
- Settings and layout saves now keep a recoverable backup. Interrupted,
  incomplete, oversized, read-only, or otherwise unsafe files are left intact
  instead of being replaced.
- Completed broad launcher and in-game memory and size cleanup, leaving more
  headroom without taking additional memory away from Sunshine.
- One Moonshine launcher supports **JP (GMSJ01), US (GMSE01), and PAL
  (GMSP01)**. Dolphin development patches are provided for all three regions.
- Existing `susamune.ini` settings, binds, records, achievements, ghosts,
  layouts, and other Moonshine data remain compatible. Keep these files in
  place when updating.

The gameplay timer implementation was not changed.
