another full regression pass. Keep your existing INI, PBs, records, ghosts,

## Checks

- [ ] **1. Boot and version:** Reach gameplay. The launcher says
  `V2.2.0 Release Candidate 3 "The House Always Wins"`, and Records says
  `Moonshine V2.2.0 RC3`.

- [ ] **2. Catalog:** Records shows **210** total achievements. The category
  totals are Times 81, Challenges 15, Course Mastery 33, Streaks 41, and
  Special 40. Browse each category and make sure the new names and descriptions
  are readable. Chuckster Change, No Kidding, and Tower Titan are absent.

- [ ] **3. PB Safety page:** Turn on one PB-blocking visual and one boss RNG
  option. Open **Settings > PB Safety**. Both appear in red, the Settings root
  reports the correct blocked count, **A** fixes one, and **X** fixes all.

- [ ] **4. PB labels in normal Settings:** Visit the normal page containing
  one of those options. A safe value is marked **PB**; an unsafe value is red
  and marked **PB OFF**. With everything fixed, PB Safety says **Ready**.

- [ ] **5. Popup category:** Earn any convenient new Times achievement, such
  as Dock Knock. Its popup shows `Times / Bronze` as well as the achievement
  name. If another tester earns a Challenge, Mastery, Streak, or Special item,
  confirm that category is named correctly too.

- [ ] **6. Ordinary eligibility:** With PB Safety showing Ready, complete one
  familiar IL and confirm PB handling is normal. Turn on hurtboxes or Ricco 2
  checkpoints and confirm the run cannot save a PB; turn it off and restart the
  IL before trying again.

- [ ] **7. Boss exceptions:** Split this across testers if useful. The House
  Always Wins may unlock with only King Boo control active, and Perfect Peter
  may unlock with only Petey controls active. Adding any unrelated PB blocker
  must prevent the achievement.

- [ ] **8. Streak and challenge spot-check:** Complete one new streak far
  enough to see its progress, and one convenient challenge such as No Safety
  Net or Lost and FLUDD. Using the forbidden move must prevent the challenge.

- [ ] **9. Ghost achievements:** If you already have suitable ghosts, check at
  least one of Mirror Match, Stranger Danger, or Dead Heat. Watching a ghost,
  using an unfinished ghost, or racing the wrong route must not unlock one.

- [ ] **10. General smoke check:** Open Settings and Records, run one normal
  ghost race or short IL, make and reload one ordinary savestate, then leave
  the stage. Menus, saving, HUD elements, and gameplay should behave as they
  did in RC2.

## Optional precision check
