# Moonshine 2.3.3 — tester checklist

Please include your system (Wii, Wii U or Dolphin), game region and build checksum with any problem. A short description or video helps. There is no need to repeat a passing check five times.

**Known testing limit:** ghost bodies currently have rendering problems in our Dolphin test setup. Mario's ghost appearance needs console testing and has not been confirmed on Wii yet.

## Ghosts

- Save a new ghost, then open the ghost list **without restarting**. It should appear alongside older ghosts. Change pages in both directions and try Refresh once; the list should stay usable.
- Record a short run using spray and hover. Restart and check that FLUDD sits on the ghost's back, changes nozzle and sprays at the right moments. Water should use Sunshine's textures; exact droplet paths and splashes will differ. Try both Shadow Mario and Piantissimo if convenient.
- Save that ghost, reboot, then race or Watch it. Try Watch 2 with two new ghosts, or a new ghost alongside an older one.
- Load an existing ghost from 2.3.2 or earlier. It should still play normally. If you already have a ghost over 10 minutes, check that too; do not record a long one just for this test.
- **Optional, on console:** choose Mario as the ghost appearance. Check full and lower opacity, then switch back to another appearance. The ghost and FLUDD should stay visible, and your live Mario should look unchanged.
- During Ghost Watch, pause, step and use freecam. Water should pause with the ghost and disappear when ghost display is off. Ghost water must not clean goop or affect Mario or enemies.

## Streaks and route changes

- Find the separate **Stageloader** and **Streaking** entries under Runs. Complete a streak attempt with an RNG control enabled; it should count.
- During a streak, save and load a state, then finish. That practice finish should neither add to nor break your streak. Restart normally and finish under the target to confirm counting resumes.
- Try **Plaza movement > Gelato Enter** with the Yoshi Plaza state. Check the Yoshi introduction plays, the short name is **GE**, and timing ends when entering Gelato.
- Finish Sandbird or Noki 6 Full Reds in a streak. A clean finish under the target should count. We have not reproduced the reported missed streaks yet; if one fails, send any reason shown, your region and active practice settings.
- With a save box open, try loading a RAM state and an SD state, plus a warp or level select. An active memory-card write must finish before the action proceeds.

## Feedback and a normal practice run

- Open **Streaking > Failure banner appearance**. Try position, size, colour and background, then save and reopen it. Visibility and duration remain beside the editor. Also try System messages and Savestate errors Off; PB/achievement popups should keep their own settings.
- Load a saved position with QFT visible, then hidden. Check for the small **TAS** label in both cases. A level split should not show doubled QFT text.
- Edit both timers through **Timer and splits > Cosmetics**. Check that your normal timer, input display and colours look right after closing the menu.
- Do one ordinary IL: check its finish time, PB and ghost. Save/load a state and try a short TAS with a checkpoint. If you have a public 2.3.1/2.3.2 SD state, load it in its original region and episode.
- In Sirena 6, check Visible goop Off looks normal, including after loading a state saved with it On.
- Notice the wait after Launch Game and an ordinary level load/restart. Report any new delay, freezing or missing assets; a stopwatch comparison is optional.

New ghosts have a 10-minute recording limit. Old ghosts are preserved. Loading-speed improvements still need measurement on Wii; report what you notice without feeling obliged to time every load.
