# Moonshine V2.2.0 Release Candidate 1

## The House Always Wins

RC1 brings the complete V2.2.0 feature set together for its final public test.
It is built from the validated Pre-Release 8 line. Full Reds, Pinna 8's HUD,
and saving after a ghost race have passed hardware testing.

## RC1 change from Pre-Release 8

- Ricco 2's transparent race checkpoints now remain visible as they approach
  the edge of the screen instead of appearing for only a few frames. Visible
  faces are kept safely inside the screen so close checkpoints cannot create
  a stretched or screen-filling shape.

## RNG practice

- Added King Boo's alternating fruit cycle and Bianco 5 Petey's no-tornado and
  N1-S1-S2-S3 route controls.
- Added five random speed bands for Ricco's cranes and a Durians-only Ricco
  fruit machine.
- Added repeatable Gelato 6 red-coin fish and blue-coin bird patterns.
- Reorganised the RNG settings into clear nested pages alongside Pattern
  Selector and Any Fruit Opens Yoshi Eggs.
- Boss assistance marks the attempt with a red HUD and `INVALID IL`. Other
  successful practice assists still prevent assisted records without turning
  the HUD red.
- Removed the unfinished Bianco Skeeter control while preserving its saved
  settings slot for compatibility.

## ILs, Streaking, and ghosts

- Added Full Reds ILs for all ten full-level secret-red routes, including Stage
  Loader and Streaking support. Full Reds keeps FLUDD through the secret and
  records separately from ordinary Secret Reds.
- Bianco 2 now records the rollout, the real Windmill/Petey movie handoff, all
  three Petey hits, and the final Shine.
- Streaking accepts any Shine available in the chosen episode while keeping
  Full Reds routes exact. Hidden and 100-coin choices remain available.
- Fixed saving a new challenger while racing a library ghost, including
  child-stage routes such as Noki 6 Secret.
- Existing records remain compatible. Only Bianco 2's newly separated movie
  timing starts fresh.

## Displays and practice tools

- Added Rollout and Dust timing displays with Layout Editor support.
- Added stationary airgrab QFT freeze and restored the classic input display.
- Added global spray-hidden fruit and coin markers with filters and optional
  labels.
- Added enemy hurtboxes with multiple styles and an Eely-teeth-only option.
- Added transparent Ricco 2 race checkpoints and fixed the Pinna 8 timer and
  balloon-counter overlap.
- Hurtboxes, hidden-item markers, and Ricco 2 checkpoints invalidate assisted
  PB and Records credit while enabled without turning the HUD red.
- Added the classic position save/load actions to Button Binds.

## Menus and release work

- Reorganised Settings into smaller nested pages with clearer names,
  descriptions, separators, and faster section navigation.
- Moonshine takes control of the pad while its menu is open, preventing
  accidental pause-menu or save-box input.
- Completed broad launcher and in-game size and memory cleanup without taking
  additional memory away from Sunshine.
- JP, US, and PAL remain supported by the same launcher.
- Existing `susamune.ini`, records, ghosts, and other durable files remain
  compatible and should be kept in place during the update.

The gameplay timer implementation was not changed.
