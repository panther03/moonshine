# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 7 — optional visual checks

These are extra checks for someone comfortable stress-testing the new visuals.
They are optional and do not need to be repeated by every tester.

- [ ] In a crowded stage, put hurtboxes behind Mario, behind the camera, at
  the edge of the screen, and extremely close to the camera. No line or solid
  face should stretch across the screen.
- [ ] In Ricco 2, view checkpoints from both ends of the course and from very
  close range. Labels and crosshairs remain readable without covering gameplay.
- [ ] In Pinna 8, watch the balloon counter with short and wide timer values.
  The retail counter stays in place and the timer remains above it.
- [ ] Try one other full-level secret-red route after Pinna 6. FLUDD stays on
  and the route can complete and PB normally.
- [ ] Browse long Ghost and Layout Editor lists while reading the help text.
  Scrolling never hides the selected row under the description.

The bird and Gelato fish pattern controls are unchanged and do not need another
round of testing for PR7.
