# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 7 — tester log

## The House Always Wins

This is a short check of the things changed after PR6. Mark each item **PASS**,
**FAIL**, or **NOT TESTED**. A short note, photo, or video is enough for a
failure. Skip anything you do not know how to test.

- [ ] **1. Boot and version:** Reach gameplay. The launcher says
  `V2.2.0 Pre-Release 7 "The House Always Wins"`, and Records says
  `Moonshine V2.2.0 PR7`.

- [ ] **2. Full Reds:** Start Pinna 6 `Full Reds`, once directly from the IL
  list and once through Stage Loader. FLUDD stays available through the secret,
  finishing the secret reds records an IL result, and a faster finish can earn
  a PB.

- [ ] **3. Bianco 2 Petey splits:** Complete the Bianco 2 IL. Petey hit 1,
  hit 2, and hit 3 each advance the split exactly once. The final Shine then
  finishes the IL.

- [ ] **4. Ghost race saving:** In Noki 6 Secret, choose a saved ghost and
  select `RACE`. Restart, finish a visibly different run, and save it. Loading
  the new recording shows the complete run instead of a short or stationary
  ghost, and the selected opponent remains available for another race.

- [ ] **5. Dust display:** Dust still appears for a dry-ground rollout. It does
  not appear at any point while Mario is in the water-sliding state, including
  the first frame of the slide.

- [ ] **6. Classic input Start:** Shrink the classic input display to its
  minimum size. Start remains easy to see at rest and clearly fills while held.

- [ ] **7. Hurtbox safety:** Turn hurtboxes on in a crowded stage and move the
  camera very close to enemies. Cycle `Wireframe`, `Transparent`, and `Solid`.
  No giant orange/green shapes or broken screen geometry should appear.

- [ ] **8. Ricco 2 checkpoints:** Enable the race checkpoints in Ricco 2.
  Their crosshairs, labels, and colours are easy to see, and camera movement
  never stretches them into screen-filling shapes.

- [ ] **9. Pinna 8 HUD:** During the roller-coaster ride, the retail balloon
  counter stays fully on-screen. The Moonshine timer sits above it rather than
  covering it.

- [ ] **10. Menu descriptions:** Open Settings and browse Gameplay, RNG,
  Savestates, Timer, HUD and Displays, Appearance, Layout Editor, Ghosts, and
  Records. The selected page or option has a short useful description and no
  row or footer is covered by that text.

- [ ] **11. General regression:** Normal ILs, Stage Loader, Streaking, the mod
  menu, input display, and the existing RNG controls still behave normally.
