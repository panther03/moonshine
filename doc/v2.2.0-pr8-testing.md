# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 8 — tester log

## The House Always Wins

This is a short retest of the PR7 failures. The input display, Dust display,
hurtboxes, and menu descriptions already passed and are not repeated here.
Mark each item **PASS**, **FAIL**, or **NOT TESTED**. A short note, photo, or
video is enough for a failure.

- [ ] **1. Boot and version:** Reach gameplay. The launcher says
  `V2.2.0 Pre-Release 8 "The House Always Wins"`, and Records says
  `Moonshine V2.2.0 PR8`.

- [ ] **2. Full Reds:** Start Pinna 6 `Full Reds`, once directly from the IL
  list and once through Stage Loader. FLUDD remains available after entering
  the secret. Finishing the eight red coins records the Full Reds result, and
  a faster finish earns a PB.

- [ ] **3. Bianco 2 FMV and Petey splits:** Start the Bianco 2 IL and perform
  the usual rollout split. The next split advances exactly once when the game
  hands off into the Windmill/Petey FMV. Petey hits 1, 2, and 3 then each
  advance exactly once, and the final Shine finishes the IL.

- [ ] **4. Pinna 8 HUD:** During the roller-coaster ride, both the timer and
  balloon counter remain fully visible. The timer is above the balloon counter
  and never covers it, including while either display animates on-screen.

- [ ] **5. Ricco 2 checkpoints:** Enable the race checkpoints. They appear as
  quiet transparent coloured volumes with their small labels. There is no
  wireframe cage, screen-filling line, flicker, or broken geometry when moving
  close to one.

- [ ] **6. Ghost race saving:** In Noki 6 Secret, choose a saved ghost and
  select `RACE`. Restart, finish a visibly different run, and save it. Loading
  the new recording shows the complete run instead of a short or stationary
  ghost, and the selected opponent remains available for another race.
