# Moonshine V2.2.0 Release Candidate 1 — optional specialist checks

These checks are for testers who already know the relevant stages. They are
optional and do not need to be repeated by every tester.

## Gelato 6 red-coin fish

- [ ] `Retail` gives normal random openings.
- [ ] Patterns 1, 2, 3, and 4 each repeat their own opening after a restart.
- [ ] Changing the fish option does not affect unrelated enemies or show
  `INVALID IL`.

## Blue-coin birds

- [ ] Test a known blue-coin bird in Delfino Plaza, Gelato Beach, and Pinna
  Park with `Retail`, then Patterns 1 through 4.
- [ ] A forced pattern repeats after a restart; `Retail` restores normal
  movement.
- [ ] Normal birds remain unchanged and the bird control does not show
  `INVALID IL`.

## Practice visuals

- [ ] Find spray-hidden fruit and coins outside Pinna. Check `Both`, `Fruit`,
  `Coins`, labels on, labels off, and `Off`. Revealed objects lose their marker.
- [ ] In Eely-Mouth, compare `All enemies` with `Eely teeth only`. Eely's body
  remains hidden, live teeth are usable, and broken teeth stop appearing.
- [ ] In Ricco 2, view every checkpoint from far away, close up, partly off the
  side of the screen, and from inside it. Visible parts remain transparent and
  steady without stretching across the screen.

## Split stress checks

- [ ] Run Bianco 2 twice. The movie handoff and all three successful Petey hits
  advance exactly once each; failed stomps do not advance anything.
- [ ] Try one Full Reds route other than the route used in the main checklist.
  FLUDD stays available and the separate Full Reds result is saved.
- [ ] Repeat the boot and one short normal IL on a second game version if one
  is available.
