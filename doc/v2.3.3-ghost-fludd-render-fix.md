# V2.3.3 ghost FLUDD visual correction

## Follow-up: measured spray motion and Mario lighting

The subsequent 40A699DE feedback exposed two further errors. Retail water
advances once per QF (four moves per Step); the prior ghost used QF/4. The new
advance uses measured retail gravity, including its pre-move velocity update,
and borrows the current nozzle's immutable size/type parameters. Droplets use
retail's velocity-stretched geometry and originate at the rendered nozzle;
both hover outlets are represented. Movement inheritance is reconstructed from
the recorded pose. These remain bounded approximations, not exact droplet or
randomness recordings. Ground hits create visual splashes, not lasting puddles.

Mario's compact material conversion had overwritten the retail diffuse channel
with lighting disabled. It now retains that channel, its light mask and material
colours, while controlling opacity separately. The model heap does not grow.
Water history uses 24 more scalar bytes within its unchanged 8 KiB reservation.

Private US `39E7A625` evidence lives in
`build/v2.3.3-light-water/candidate-runtime/motion-proof.json`, with the measured
retail baseline in `../runtime/retail-spray-cadence.json`. The natural ghost had
38 active visual particles, including 22 floor splashes. Four executed water
motion tests and four material tests passed. The full Mario lighting screenshot
was not completed; the same fake-MEM2 body limitation below applies. The older
25-unit round-droplet description below records the preceding iteration.

The hollow tank came from hiding three retail tank materials. The extended
pump came from drawing FLUDD's bind pose instead of its retracted resting pose.
The ghost now retains all six tank shapes. Its private joint transforms are
evaluated once from the already loaded `wg_house` animation's final frame;
neither Mario's animation frame nor his model data is changed. At full opacity,
attachment callbacks preserve retail glass/water blend and depth rules. Lower
ghost opacity still enables blending without depth writes.

Ghost spray uses Sunshine's water-mask and specular textures with separate
blue-base and additive-highlight passes. Combining the highlight with the
base's low alpha made the previous spray too faint. Droplets now use a 25-unit
half-size, comparable to the observed retail spray particle size of about 50
units. The existing 64 visual particles per runner remain approximations from
recorded nozzle, aim and firing observations; this does not record every retail
particle or call gameplay water, RNG, sound, goop or hit responses. The second
draw pass cannot advance particles again: the consumed visual-QF guard gives
it a zero time step.

Each visible ghost submits an ordinary projected circle to Sunshine's bounded
shadow manager. The corrected request ABI is 36 bytes. This is draw-only ground
projection, not a collision actor or platform interaction.

## Validation and limits

- The actual attachment callback executes against GX state mocks in
  `scripts/test_ghost_fludd_render.py`: full-opacity native blend preservation,
  transparent no-depth-write behavior and inactive callbacks all pass.
- All three emulator regions compile/link after the final rendering changes.
- Private US runtime image `D6B7F0E3` was generated and independently checked in
  `build/v2.3.3-water-fix/bps-proof/verification.json`. PADRead is replaced only
  in the private image for controller input. Its natural Bianco recording has
  89 firing observations and is never edited by the water test.
- `runtime/new-water-visible.png` shows the closed complete tank, visible spray
  highlights and ghost ground shadow. `tank-opacity-0..3.png` checks all four
  opacity choices. Water off/on restoration changes 1,543 stable pixels in the
  spray region; its scalar state restores exactly and survives menu open/close.
- Private tank allocation remains 33,436 bytes plus 7,476 for the spray nozzle.
  No model heap, attachment reservation, particle reservation, recording limit
  or savestate capacity changes.

These Dolphin pictures deliberately hide only the ghost body's shape packets:
its existing GPU model matrices live in Dolphin's fake MEM2 aperture, which
retail GX address masking cannot access correctly. Attachments, water and
shadows are drawn by the production paths. This is scoped visual evidence,
not a claim that the complete ghost body or Wii rendering was verified here.
