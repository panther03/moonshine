The fish, bird, boss, crane, fruit-machine, hidden-item, hurtbox, and Ricco 2
checkpoint features are unchanged from RC1 and do not need another full pass.

an unfinished RC1 check. The six checks in `TESTING.md` are the complete RC2
pass.
