# Moonshine V2.2.0 Release Candidate 3 — optional specialist checks

The existing fish, bird, crane, fruit-machine, Petey, and King Boo patterns are
unchanged. They do not need another full pattern pass.

The only optional RNG checks for RC3 are the achievement exceptions:

- **The House Always Wins** may count with King Boo control and no other PB
  blocker.
- **Perfect Peter** may count with either or both Petey controls and no other PB
  blocker.

The ten checks in `TESTING.md` are the complete normal RC3 pass.
