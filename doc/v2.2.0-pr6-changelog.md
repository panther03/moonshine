# Moonshine V2.2.0 Pre-Release 6

## The House Always Wins

PR6 focuses on the PR5 tester findings and adds the requested practice visuals.

### ILs, Stage Loader, and Streaking

- Added ten separate full-level secret-red routes: Bianco 3, Bianco 6, Ricco 4,
  Gelato 1, Pinna 2, Pinna 6, Sirena 2, Sirena 4, Noki 6, and Pianta 5.
- Added those routes to ILs, normal Stage Loader, and Streaking beside their
  courses without changing the identities of existing entries.
- Restored hidden and 100-coin Shines to the Streaking list.
- A normal episode streak can count its full-secret red Shine, while a selected
  Full Reds streak remains exact.
- Placed Gelato GBS with Gelato in every relevant level list.
- Repaired Bianco 2 so all three Petey hits can advance its split sequence.

### Practice displays

- Expanded spray-hidden fruit and coin markers from Pinna to every stage that
  uses those objects.
- Added `Both`, `Fruit`, and `Coins` filters plus a separate label toggle.
- Expanded enemy hurtboxes to `Off`, `Wireframe`, `Transparent`, and `Solid`,
  with `All enemies` and `Eely teeth only` targets.
- Suppressed Eely-Mouth's distracting body volume while keeping each live tooth.
- Added visible retail race-checkpoint volumes for Ricco 2.
- Hidden-item markers, hurtboxes, and Ricco checkpoint assistance invalidate
  competitive attempt credit while active.
- Moved Pinna 8's balloon counter clear of the Moonshine timer without changing
  the timer code.

### PR5 repairs

- Fixed `RACE` loading so the first clean restart records the complete new
  challenger instead of saving the short pre-load prefix.
- Fixed Dust suppression on FLUDD-created sliding water as well as wet stage
  collision.
- Made the tiny classic-input Start button clearly hollow at rest and solid
  while pressed.
- Expanded PB, target, and playlist storage for the new entries while retaining
  older files automatically.
