# Moonshine 2.3.3 — this update's checks

One check per item is enough. Include your region and launcher checksum if
something goes wrong.

- **PAL Fast Text:** talk to an NPC. It should show `!!!`. Normal text should
  still work after turning Fast Text off.
- **Soft reset:** start a streak, then soft reset the console. After choosing
  your file, the streak should have ended.
- **States:** save three different positions in Delfino Plaza and load each.
  Replace one state and check that the other two still load correctly.
- **Timings:** if recording, time saves from timer freeze to timer movement;
  time loads from freeze to Mario's first visible frame. Include all three
  slots and whether you were replacing a state. The previous Wii loads were
  0.334 s, 0.334 s and 0.500 s. Please say whether loads 1/2 were timed before
  or after saving slot 3; making room for it can affect the earlier slots.
- **Level loading, optional:** repeat your Sirena 4 route if convenient. No
  need to retest the ghost appearance or other already-confirmed fixes.
